## Stage 0 deliverables (revised)

Think of Stage 0 as producing **SOW inputs**, not internal discovery docs.

### D1 — Deal Context & Value Summary (lightweight)

* Business problem, why now, expected outcomes (not deep KPI work yet)
* Used to align sponsor + justify investment

### D2 — Scope Definition Pack (SOW-grade)

* In/Out scope, assumptions, dependencies, constraints
* Phasing approach (MVP/Wave 1), clear exclusions
* Acceptance approach (what “done” means)

### D3 — Solution Approach (pre-sales architecture level)

* Target architecture, major integrations, environment approach
* Non-functional constraints (security, compliance, data)

### D4 — Delivery Feasibility Review (internal audit)

* Delivery Leadership review: delivery approach, risks, resourcing assumptions, timeline realism
* “Approved to sell” checkpoint

### D5 — SOW (the formal output)

* Robust scope protection, commercial structure, timeline guardrails
* Change control mechanism and acceptance language
* We’ll build the SOW template later as you said

### D6 — Sales → Delivery Handoff Pack (the real Stage 0 → Stage 1 bridge)

This is the **internal contract**. Without it, Stage 1 starts blind.

## Gate change: Stage 0 → Stage 1 = “SOW Signed + Handoff Completed”

### Gate 1 (redefined) — Commercial & Operational Authorization

**PASS only if:**

1. **Client signed SOW** (commercial authorization)
2. **Sales→Delivery handoff completed** (operational authorization)
3. **Delivery Lead accepts ownership** (explicit, not implied)
4. **Key assumptions/risks logged** and owned

This is the right gate for consulting reality.

---

## Sales → Delivery Handoff process (what it must contain)

This is the piece you said is “most important internally.” Agreed.

### Handoff Pack sections (minimum viable)

1. **Deal snapshot**

   * Client objectives, sponsor, stakeholders, timeline drivers
2. **What was sold**

   * Scope boundaries, phases, deliverables, acceptance approach
3. **Commercial terms that impact delivery**

   * FP/T&M model, change control, SLAs, milestones, invoicing triggers
4. **Assumptions + dependencies + constraints**

   * Especially client responsibilities and access lead times
5. **Risks and “known unknowns”**

   * What could break the plan and how we’ll mitigate
6. **Solution outline**

   * Architecture highlights, integrations, data, environments
7. **Staffing expectations**

   * Named roles (even if not named people), ramp plan, client SME commitments
8. **First 2 weeks plan**

   * Kickoff agenda, workshop plan, access checklist, decision calendar

### Handoff ritual (don’t skip this)

* 60–90 min meeting with **AE + Pre-Sales + Delivery Lead + Architect + PM (if assigned)**
* Output: **Delivery Lead signs acceptance** + lists any immediate risks/conditions
